About Us

Business Permits & Licensing Division


The Business Permits and Licensing Division (BPLD) is the office of the City Government of Cagayan de Oro responsible for the issuance, renewal, and administration of business permits and licenses within the city.

Located at the BOSS Office (Business One-Stop Shop) inside the Cagayan de Oro City Hall Compound, we are committed to providing fast, transparent, and accessible services to all business owners and applicants.

We serve as the primary gateway for anyone looking to legally operate a business within the jurisdiction of Cagayan de Oro City, ensuring compliance with local ordinances and national regulations.

Mission

🎯 Our Mission

To provide a simplified and streamlined system for the processing and issuance of business permits and other licenses within the jurisdiction of Cagayan de Oro City, ensuring compliance with all rules and regulations governing business operations, and promoting growth and development of the city's economy.

Vision

🌟 Our Vision

To be the leading Local Government Unit in the country in providing efficient business permit and licensing services. To be recognized for our excellent customer-centric approach, prompt processes, and commitment to ensuring the welfare of the public through innovative practices.
